# THE GHOST ARMY — script v1 (English, 2026-11-17)

Family 2 of Rick's pattern: **the trick with a last-line twist** (the last sentence says who was really fooled).
Target: ~220 words, ~60 s at Rick's pace (3.7 words/s). Present tense from sentence 2. No names, no dates in the voice (facts in FACTS.md).
Tone tag on each line = direction for the voice (Fish Audio).

| # | Line | Tone | Beat / fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 HOOK | Four soldiers pick up a thirty-ton tank… and set it down in a field like a suitcase. | [calm, amused] | the strange act + one exact detail (F4) |

About 225 words, roughly 60-62 s.
The mocker (the sergeant, line 10) gets his payoff on line 14: the real army crosses thanks to the "balloons".
Last frame: the old soldier's grandchild lifts the deflated tank skin out of an attic trunk. It rhymes with frame 0 (lifting a tank), so the loop closes.
