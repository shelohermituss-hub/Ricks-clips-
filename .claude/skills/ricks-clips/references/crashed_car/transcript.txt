He crashed his car right into his own invention, on purpose! You see, he was a retired race car driver, and after a bad crash, he vowed to make the racetrack safer for all. So he started working on barrels that could stop a car right in its tracks. Sure enough, the first prototype didn't go well, but once he swapped out the wooden barrels for plastic ones, things started looking better. But the final test was to drive his own car into them at 70 miles per hour. His neighbor saw him floor it in his own driveway and throw himself onto the floor right before impact. And the crazy part? It worked! Because the barrels hold more sand the further back you go, when a car hits the first one, it bursts the sand everywhere, and has to shove it out of the way. Then the next barrel, and the next, all the way until the car stops at a soft halt. So the next time you see these yellow things on the highway, remember...
