He drilled 82 holes into the wood, because down the hill an army is marching straight for his fort, and while everyone else sharpens swords, he's in the corner still drilling. His commander asks, are we fighting them or grating cheese? But it gets weirder. He bolts the wood onto a cart, and into every hole goes something long and thin, wrapped in paper, each with a string. Then he twists all the strings into one rope nobody's allowed to touch. One soldier reaches for it, and he slaps his hand so hard the whole fort hears it. That night, it pours. So he throws his coat over the cart and lies on top of it like a human umbrella. One drop gets in there, and we're all dead. The next morning, ladders slam against the walls and the army charges. Everyone screams at him to do something, but he just watches them get closer and closer, until they're packed shoulder to shoulder, close enough to see their teeth. That's when he rips off the coat and lights the rope. The spark races down it and splits into 82 tiny flames, and each one hits a tube of gunpowder strapped to an arrow, turning every one of them into a rocket. All 82 blast off the cart at once, flying every which way. But with the army packed that tight, not a single one misses.
