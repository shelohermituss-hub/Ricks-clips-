He was drinking a can of soda when suddenly a plane door burst open, sucking him and 19 others out mid -air. Whilst everyone else was panicking, he was looking for one thing only, a haystack. But the air pressure was too much, and he passed out after a thousand feet. The next day, he woke up in a hospital bed with some bruises and a concussion, and the doctor told him, we don't know how you survived, but man, I would buy a lottery ticket if I were you. You see, this guy was the unluckiest, luckiest man in the world, and this wasn't the first time he'd survived something like this. A year ago, he was taking a train to Alaska, when suddenly the tracks split apart and the train derailed into an ice -cold lake. He swam to shore with a sprained arm and brain freeze. Five years later, he started his car right outside a fire station, and the whole thing burst into flames. Talk about a fast delivery. Ten years later, he was driving his car up the Grand Canyon. He turned a sharp corner, and suddenly a truck came out of nowhere in front of him. He made a split -second decision to swerve his car into the railings. It smashed through and launched him out the side door. He fell 50 feet before grabbing onto a tree on the side of the cliff. Fast forward 15 years, he remembered what that doctor told him, bought a lottery ticket, and ended up hitting the jackpot for $1 million.
